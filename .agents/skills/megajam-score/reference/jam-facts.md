# Epic AI MegaJam 2026 — gathered facts

Collected 2026-11-13 (jam day 2). Primary source: the itch.io jam page
<https://itch.io/jam/2026-epic-ai-megajam>. The Official Rules PDF (epicgames.box.com) could not be read as text;
details marked *(secondary)* come from third-party guides that quote it and are unverified.

## Theme: DIRECTOR'S CUT
"Your vision, your edits, your final cut." The blurb talks about features that got shelved or scoped out, and adds
that when you use AI agents "you're directing those too … decide what makes the cut."
Readings judges will likely see a lot of (so less "fresh"): film / movie-set games, literal cutting, a
director character yelling "cut". Fresher readings: editing reality or a timeline, re-cutting events, choosing what
stays in the final version, alternate takes, the player as editor of a story.

## Judging (official)
A panel of judges scores each qualified entry **1 to 5** on each of seven criteria. **No weights are published**, so
treat them as equal (max 35). No community vote. Scores are not released (history).

| # | Criterion | Official one-liner (paraphrased) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ambition & innovation | Something you couldn't usually pull off in a 4-day jam: new mechanic, bold art style, or bigger scope |
| 2 | Theme | A fresh, clever take on the theme |
| 3 | Fun factor | Fun and satisfying to play |
| 4 | Gameplay | Solid, original mechanics that feel good |
| 5 | Visuals | A strong, consistent look, photoreal or stylized |
| 6 | Level design & progression | Spaces that support the gameplay and guide the player |
| 7 | Audio | Sound and music that add to the experience |

- AI use is "for context only": it neither raises nor lowers the score *(secondary, rules §10)*.
- Third-party / Fab / pre-jam assets are allowed if licensed and **listed**, but are **not scored**; judges score
  original jam work *(secondary, rules §15c; same rule in 2023/2025)*. AI output made during the jam is not a
  "sourced asset".
- Custom gameplay beyond starter templates is required *(secondary, §7e)*.

## Prizes (cash)
Main (one per entry), 1st / 2nd / 3rd:
- **Best Overall — Unreal Engine: $7,500 / $5,000 / $2,500** ← our track
- Best Overall — UEFN: same
- Best Student Project: same (must be enrolled in higher education)

Modifiers (stack one main + one modifier, per secondary sources):
- Best Team $5,000 (2+ people) · Solo Driver $2,500 (solo dev)
- Render Royalty $2,500 (best visuals); + NVIDIA RTX 5090 for a UE entry using an NvRTX feature, DLSS 4.5 or ACE;
  + $2,500 OpenAI credits if OpenAI models used in dev or gameplay
- Pocket Powerhouse $2,500 (best mobile, UE)
- **Glass Box $2,500 — best dev log showing how the game was built** (dev log link required). Fits an AI/MCP-heavy
  workflow very well.
- Walkie-Talkie (UEFN only)
- OpenAI Award (Best Overall UE): $5,000 credits for the best use of OpenAI models/products (answer the question at
  submission).
- Hyper3D Rodin cash pool $10k split among winners that list Rodin in "Tools you used".

Development Continuation Grants (separate, "standout" entries): UE $50k / $25k / $10k + Fab credit.

## Submission checklist (hard gates — any miss = zero chance)
- [ ] Packaged **development** build (Win10/11, macOS, Android or iOS), or Web build via "UE 5.9 MegaJam Preview"
      (Shipping). Unpackaged projects are rejected.
- [ ] Zip named `TeamName_GameName`, < 1 GB (else use butler); uploaded to your own itch account, then
      **"Submit your project"** to the jam (joining is not submitting).
- [ ] 30–60 s gameplay video link.
- [ ] Team name, each member's name + Epic account email, game name.
- [ ] All non-jam assets (Fab, marketplace, pre-jam prompts/scripts/skills) listed with source/license.
- [ ] Nothing built before Nov 12 besides declared tooling; no trademarks/logos except the UE logo; no others' IP.
- [ ] Runs without extra software. Every mesh has a non-default material (rule in 2023/2025, likely still applies).
- [ ] Submitted before Nov 15 5:59 PM PST. Not entered in both UE and UEFN.
- Optional but worth it: dev log link (Glass Box), "Tools you used" (partner prizes), OpenAI / NVIDIA questions.

## History (what past jams teach)
| Year | Theme | Entries | UE winners |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2015 | Standing on the Shoulders of Giants | ~170 | categories: Addiction / Philosophy / Eye Candy |
| 2019 | Down to Earth | 460+ | 1 Planted, 2 Cosmic Catastrophe, 3 Careful Descent |
| 2021 | — | 504 | 1 BotiBoi (Purple Ray; became a commercial UE5 platformer) |
| 2022 | — | 509 | 1 Fregward: Spirit Warden (Catfrog) |
| 2023 | — | — | 1 Rolling Back, 2 Neo-Siege, 3 The Lost Slipper |
| 2024 | no jam | | |
| 2025 | Here we go again | ~490 (540 teams) | 1 These Four Walls, 2 Phantom's Manor, 3 Renaissance |

2025 criteria were the same minus "Ambition & innovation" (6 × 1–5): theme, fun, visuals, level design, audio,
gameplay.

Patterns and lessons:
- **Field size.** ~500 entries total; the UE Best Overall pool is probably 300–450 entries. Top 3 = top ~1%.
- **Judges don't play everything deeply.** 2015: ~30 min per game expected; 2025: community said judges played only
  a fraction. First impressions — the video, the itch page, the first 60 seconds — carry the score.
- **Visual-first bias** is a recurring community complaint (2020, 2025): strong art wins more than technical depth.
- **Audio matters on the rubric** (1/7 of the score) even though one 2025 modifier winner had none; a silent game
  caps itself.
- **Small and polished beats big and broken**; a complete loop with an ending is the floor for top-10.
- **Sponsor categories** are partly picked by sponsor preference.
- Winners take 2–5 months to announce; scores never published.
