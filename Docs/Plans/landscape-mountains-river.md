# Landscape: mountains, river, rolling land

Goal: the flat dark slab around the colony becomes a low-poly Nordic landscape — a snow-capped mountain range to the
north that closes the fjord, rolling wooded land west and south, and a small river along the south side that runs
into the sea — with matching ambience (river, mountain wind, forest birds, ravens, owls). The colony itself stays on
level ground with three low grassy knolls that units walk over.
Out of scope: UE Landscape actor, Water plugin, Megascans / Fab assets, Niagara, collision, pathing (`Blocked`
grid), rules, economy, scene scripts, music, `Map_Saga.umap` edits, the ocean (`TSCOcean.*` is read, not changed).

Step order: wave 1 = S1 and S2 in parallel; then S3 → S4 → S5 → S6 → S7, one at a time.

### Design

1. **One height function, owned by C++** (`ATSCTerrain::HeightAt`), sampled to build a flat-shaded procedural mesh.
   No material tricks: per-facet colour comes from putting each triangle into one of a few mesh sections, each with
   an `M_SagaFlat` MID (the project's standard look). Low-poly facets of ~300 units read well from the game camera.
2. Formula (world Z; `N(p)` = smooth value noise 0..1, `Fbm` = 4 octaves, `Ridged` = 4 octaves of `(1-|2N-1|)²`;
   `ss` = smoothstep). All numbers are starting values, tuned by eye in S7:
   - `d` = distance outside the flat rectangle: `dx = max(-4800 - x, 0)`, `dy = max(|y| - 3400, 0)`, `d = hypot`.
   - Rolling land: `Roll = ss(0, 2500, d) * (30 + 420 * Fbm(p / 5200))`, then clamped to ≤ 650.
   - Mountains: `n = (-y - 3600) + 900 * (Fbm(p / 6000 + 17) - 0.5)` (distance north of the foot line);
     `Foot = ss(0, 5500, n)`;
     `Coast = lerp(1 - ss(-2500, 1600, x), 1, ss(2600, 4800, n))` (near the colony's latitude the range tapers down
     to the beach; further north it carries on across the water as the headland that closes the fjord);
     `Mount = pow(Foot, 1.6) * Coast * (900 + 5200 * Ridged(p / 7000))`.
   - `Land = -7 + Roll + Mount`.
   - Sea floor: `Sea = ss(1800, 2100, x) * (1 - ss(2600, 4800, n))`; `Z = lerp(Land, -60, Sea)` (under the beach and
     the opaque sea everywhere except where the mountains cross the water).
   - River (below), applied last and only where `Sea < 0.5`.
3. **River.** Centreline `Ry(x) = 4600 + 250 * sin(x / 1700) + 150 * sin(x / 610 + 1.3) + 0.10 * max(-x - 5000, 0)`,
   for x from -45000 to the waterline. Water level `Rz(x) = 9 + 0.004 * max(1000 - x, 0)` (a gentle fall to the
   sea; 9 is 5 above the beach top so it does not z-fight). With `r = |y - Ry(x)|`:
   `Z = lerp(Z, Rz + 12, 1 - ss(420, 1200, r))` (the land comes down to low banks), then
   `Z -= 72 * (1 - ss(200, 420, r))` (the bed). Both river terms are multiplied by `ss(0, 300, d)` (`d` from §2), so the
   flat rectangle stays exactly flat (S2 finding: without the mask the south edge rose to Z +37 through the tiles; the
   centreline was also moved south from 4150 so the water ribbon never reaches the rectangle). The water is a flat ribbon mesh of half-width **420** at `Rz`
   (280 for x > 1700, where it crosses the beach, and it ends 90 past `ShoreXAt`): it is wider than the bed on
   purpose, so the visible bank line is where the faceted terrain cuts through the water plane — irregular for free.
4. **River material `M_SagaRiver`**: opaque Default Lit like the sea, built by script, reusing `T_OceanPatterns` and
   the sea's colours. Foam net scrolls downstream along the ribbon's V; the mouth fades to foam white so it meets the
   sea's shore foam.
5. **Scatter**: pines as `UInstancedStaticMeshComponent`s (one per pine mesh), boulders as one ISM of the engine
   cube in stone colour. Deterministic (`FRandomStream`), placed on the height function.
6. **Sound**: three 2D loops mixed by camera focus (river near the river, wind near the mountains and when zoomed
   out, birds by day over the woods) plus rare one-shots (raven by day, owl at night).
7. **Knolls** (S5): low domes `Z = -4 + h * (1 - (r/R)²)²` drawn by the terrain actor in meadow green; units stand
   at `max(0, that)`.

struct FTSCOceanInput;   // include "TSCOcean.h" in the .cpp
class ATSCOcean;

/** The land around the colony: a faceted heightfield (flat under the play area, mountains north, rolling land west and south), the river and the land ambience. */
UCLASS()
class ATSCTerrain : public AActor
{
	GENERATED_BODY()
public:
	ATSCTerrain();
	/** Builds the meshes. Ocean gives the waterline (may be null: the straight line X = 2200 is used). */
	void Build(const ATSCOcean* Ocean);
	/** Pushes time and ash to the materials, mixes the ambience. Call once per tick. Ash = ATSCGameMode::AshAmount. */
	void Sim(float Dt, const FTSCOceanInput& In, float Ash);
	/** World Z of the land at P (exactly -7 inside the flat rectangle; river bed and sea floor included). */
	float HeightAt(const FVector2D& P) const;
	/** River centreline Y at world X, and its water level. */
	static float RiverY(float X);
	static float RiverZ(float X);
	/** 0 on the river centreline .. 1 at 3500 units or more from it (for the sound mix). */
	float RiverProximity(const FVector2D& P) const;

	UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere, Category = "Saga|Terrain") float RiverVolume = 0.5f;
	UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere, Category = "Saga|Terrain") float WindVolume = 0.35f;
	UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere, Category = "Saga|Terrain") float BirdsVolume = 0.3f;
	UPROPERTY(EditAnywhere, Category = "Saga|Terrain") float SfxVolume = 0.4f;
private:
	UPROPERTY() TObjectPtr<UProceduralMeshComponent> Land;
	UPROPERTY() TObjectPtr<UProceduralMeshComponent> River;
	UPROPERTY() TArray<TObjectPtr<UMaterialInstanceDynamic>> LandMIDs;   // one per colour band
	UPROPERTY() TObjectPtr<UMaterialInstanceDynamic> RiverMID;
	TArray<FLinearColor> LandColors;   // living colour per band
	float ShoreX = 2200.f, LastAsh = -1.f;
	TWeakObjectPtr<const ATSCOcean> OceanRef;
};

- File-static noise: `Hash(int x, int y)` → 0..1, `N(FVector2D)` bilinear with smoothstep weights, `Fbm`, `Ridged`
  exactly as Design §2. Put every tunable number of Design §2–§3 in one `namespace` block of `constexpr` at the top
  with a one-line comment each; S7 tunes them there.
- `HeightAt`: Design §2 then §3. `RiverProximity`: `saturate(|P.Y - RiverY(P.X)| / 3500)` inverted as documented
  (return 1 far away).
- `Build`:
  - Grid lines. X: -45000, -37000, -29000, -21000, -16500, -13500, -12000, then every 300 up to 6000, then 7500,
    9000, 12000, 16500, 21000, 29000, 37000, 45000. Y: -45000, -37000, -29000, -21000, -16500, -13500, -12000, then
    every 300 up to 3300, every 150 from 3450 to 5550 (river resolution), every 300 from 5850 to 7050, then 8500,
    10000, 13000, 17000, 21000, 30000.
  - For each quad make two triangles with **their own three vertices** and the face normal (flat shading), winding
    so the top faces +Z (same order the ocean grid uses — the surface must be visible from above). Skip a quad when
    all four corners are below -50 (sea floor: never seen).
  - Band per triangle, from its centroid height `h`, its slope (`Normal.Z`) and a hash of the triangle index for
    tone variation. Bands and living colours (`Pal::` from `TSCBuilders.h`):
    0 lowland `Pal::ForestFloor * 0.8` (must equal the old base colour — it borders the tiles);
    1 / 2 hill green `Pal::ForestFloor * 1.05` and `Pal::Grass * 0.9` (h > 25, picked by hash);
    3 / 4 rock `Pal::StoneDark * 1.1` and `Pal::Stone * 0.75` (`Normal.Z < 0.80`, or h > 1700; by hash);
    5 / 6 snow `(0.62, 0.68, 0.74)` and `(0.74, 0.79, 0.84)` (h > 2500 + 500 * noise and `Normal.Z > 0.55`; by hash);
    7 bank / river bed `Pal::Shore * 0.8` (centroid within 520 of the river centreline and h < RiverZ + 16);
    8 road `Pal::Dirt * 0.8` (x < -4800, |y| < 220, band 0 or 1 otherwise: the forest road carries on west).
    Rock wins over green, snow over rock, bank and road over everything.
  - One `CreateMeshSection_LinearColor` per band (skip empty ones), no collision. Each section gets a MID of
    `/Game/Vikings/Environment/M_SagaFlat.M_SagaFlat` with `Color` set (skip the material if missing). Component:
    no collision, `SetCastShadow(false)`, `bAffectDistanceFieldLighting = false`, receives shadows.
  - River ribbon (`River` component): sample x from -45000 to `ShoreAt(RiverY(2200)) + 90` (via `Ocean->ShoreXAt`,
    or 2200 + 90 without an ocean) every 150 for x > -12000 and every 1500 before. At each sample the tangent of the
    centreline gives the across direction; two vertices at ± half-width (420; 280 for x > 1700, linear blend from
    1400), Z = `RiverZ(x)`, normal +Z, `UV0 = (0 or 1, arc length / 600)` with V growing **downstream**, vertex
    colour R = 1 on the last three samples (mouth fade) else 0. Winding up-facing. No collision, no shadow.
    Material: `/Game/Vikings/Environment/River/M_SagaRiver.M_SagaRiver` (`LOAD_NoWarn | LOAD_Quiet`), fallback
    `M_SagaFlat` with `Color = (0.035, 0.20, 0.24)` and one `LogSaga` warning, exactly like the ocean does.
- `Sim` in this step: `RiverMID` scalars `RiverTime = In.Time`, `Ash`; when `|Ash - LastAsh| > 0.01` set each land
  MID's `Color` to `lerp(LandColors[i], ATSCPrimActor::Ashify(LandColors[i]), Ash * 0.7)` and store `LastAsh`.
- `PrimaryActorTick.bCanEverTick = false`; a root scene component.
